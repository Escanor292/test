---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea until every branch of the design tree is resolved.
---

Interview the user relentlessly until shared understanding. Map as a design tree.

Work in rounds: ask the whole frontier (questions whose prerequisites are settled) at once. Number them, give a recommended answer, word so "yes" accepts the recommendation.

Finding facts is your job (dispatch sub-agents). Decisions are the user's.

Done when the frontier is empty. Do not act until the user confirms shared understanding.

On this platform, pay special attention to payment, settlement, KYC, digital warehouse, and membership edges.
